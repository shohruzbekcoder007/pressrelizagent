# Regulations Reference: siat-verification

The `siat-verification` agent operates under the regulatory frameworks of the Statistics Agency under the President of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

## Primary Legal Authorities

### 1. Agency-wide AI Governance (Order No. 56)
- **Order**: Order No. 56 dated July 3, 2026 — *Regulation on the Use of Artificial Intelligence in the State Statistical System*.
- **Federal SSOT**: `gis-ai-dept/knowledge/regulations/2026-07-03-ai-use-regulation-statistics-system.md`
- **Key Directives**: All AI-assisted statistical verification systems must maintain zero-hallucination guardrails, audit logging, and human-in-the-loop review for public releases.

### 2. Information Security and Zero-PII Policy
- All public verification workflows must ensure that no non-public or individual microdata is exposed.
- Only aggregated, published statistical facts from SIAT are permitted in automated outputs.

### 3. State Committee Press Office Guidelines
- Official press releases and public social media postings must adhere to verified data formats and mandatory citation of the official statistical register.
